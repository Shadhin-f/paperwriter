# Code of conduct

PaperWriter is built by people who write, research and teach. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project lives, to feel welcome and respected.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, LaTeX or the codebase.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain the why behind a review comment.
- Accept decisions gracefully, and help the project move forward.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind, including about age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
  and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a request to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@Shadhin-f](https://github.com/Shadhin-f), privately, using the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

Maintainers will review every report and respond as they see fit. That may be a private conversation, a public warning,
removing content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
